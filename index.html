How to read it

  • Horizontal is economics: redistribution, unions and public provision on the left; markets, low tax and business on the right.
  • Vertical is authority: nationalism, immigration restriction, moral regulation and state power at the top; civil liberties, anti-censorship and anti-state positions at the bottom. Being offensive is not the same as being authoritarian.
  • Genre is shown by colour and shape together, so it survives colour-blindness and print. It is a filing decision per person, not a claim.
  • Evidence: self-description counts most, then endorsements and donations, then sustained activism, then interviews. Lyrics count least.
  • Precision: high-confidence marks are good to about ±1 unit, medium ±1.5, low ±2.5. Marks that would sit on top of each other are nudged apart by less than that.
  • Sourcing: placements marked not yet sourced come from general knowledge and are being checked one by one. Anything that cannot be sourced will be removed.
  • Sample: hand-picked and mostly Anglo-American. An American Democratic endorsement lands centre-left here, which is why so much pop clusters in one place.
